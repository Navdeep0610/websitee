1. Pickup & Delivery

What are the daily laundry pickup and delivery slots?

Our hostel pickup vans run twice daily:

  • Morning Slot: 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM (ideal before classes begin)
  • Evening Slot: 5:30 PM to 7:30 PM (after campus hours)

Deliveries occur during the evening slot once your washing and steam pressing cycle is complete.

Where do I hand over my clothes in the hostel?

You can either hand your bag to our collection executive at your hostel lobby / ground floor desk, or request direct doorstep room pickup via the Request Pickup form.

What if I am in class or outside during the delivery slot?

If you are unavailable in your room, our staff will leave your safely sealed laundry package with your hostel warden / caretaker desk, or arrange a re-delivery during the next morning slot at no extra charge.

How long does regular laundry turnaround take?

Regular daily wear takes 24 to 48 hours. Heavy winter items like blankets and quilts require 48 hours to ensure complete deep drying and sanitization.

2. Rates, Pricing & Payments

How much does blanket, comforter, or quilt cleaning cost?

Blankets, comforters, and heavy quilts are charged at a flat rate of ₹200 per piece.

This includes heavy-duty commercial drum washing, anti-bacterial sanitization, complete thermal drying, and vacuum packaging.

What is covered under the regular student hostel quota?

Resident students receive an allowance of 30 regular clothing items per month (shirts, t-shirts, trousers, jeans, kurta, and bedsheets) covered directly under your semester hostel fee. Items beyond 30 are billed at standard pay-per-piece rates (₹15/piece).

What payment methods are accepted for extra charges?

We accept:

  • UPI / QR Code: Scan and pay on delivery (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
  • Cash: Payable to delivery staff or at the Ground Floor Block 34 Helpdesk.
  • Campus Student Card: Deducted from your student amenity wallet.
Is there an express / same-day delivery service available?

Yes! Express 24-hour turnaround is available for urgent requirements (e.g. campus placements, interviews) with a nominal surcharge of ₹50 per bundle. Please select the urgent checkbox or inform the helpdesk while booking.

3. Laundry Process & Fabric Care

How are my clothes separated and washed?

All clothes are sorted into distinct categories before entering the machine: whites, light colors, dark clothes, and heavy fabrics. We use commercial grade, hypoallergenic detergents and fabric conditioners that protect garment colors.

Can I send delicate or dry-clean-only clothes?

Please notify us if an item requires delicate gentle cold wash or dry cleaning (e.g. blazers, silk, heavy embroidery). Such items are tagged separately and handled individually.

What should I do before submitting my laundry bag?

To avoid delays and damage, please follow this checklist:

  • Empty all pockets (remove coins, ID cards, pens, earbuds, cash).
  • Tie drawstrings and fasten zips to prevent snagging.
  • Count total pieces and retain your booking acknowledgment.

4. Tracking, Claims & Support

How do I track my order if I forgot my registration number?

You can track using your registered 10-digit mobile number on our Tracking page, or contact our WhatsApp support desk at +91 12345 67890 with your hostel name and room number.

What is your policy if an item is damaged or misplaced?

Every bag undergoes automated barcode tagging and piece count logging. In the rare event of damage or loss, report the issue within 24 hours of delivery. After verification with CCTV sorting logs, verified claims are compensated as per university laundry norms up to 10x the service charge.
