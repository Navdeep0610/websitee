Laundry process & fabric care details

Every piece of clothing handed to FreshFold follows a strict 5-stage hygiene and quality protocol designed for university students.

1

Hostel Collection & Tagging

Bags are collected from room doors or hostel desks, weighed, and labeled with unique student registration tags.

2

Fabric & Color Sorting

Clothes are strictly segregated into whites, darks, and delicate fabrics to prevent color bleeding or fiber friction.

3

Sanitized Machine Wash

Washed in commercial laundry drums using certified hypoallergenic detergents and fabric conditioners at controlled temperatures.

4

Thermal Dry & Steam Press

Clothes are tumble-dried with hot air to eliminate germs, then steam-ironed for crisp, wrinkle-free campus wear.

5

Sealed Pack & Room Delivery

Final item count verification against your ticket, neatly folded, waterproof sealed, and delivered back to your hostel room.

Student Preparation Checklist

  • Check all pockets: Ensure pens, coins, paper notes, USB drives, or campus ID cards are removed.
  • Heavy Winterwear & Blankets: Handed over separately for the specialized ₹200 deep-wash and sun drying cycle (48 hours).
  • Delicate & Dry-clean Garments: Please flag blazers, embroidered traditional attire, or woolens so they receive gentle cycle treatment.
  • Labeling: For identical hostel items (e.g. bedsheets), marking your initials helps instantaneous sorting.
